Participant privacy

This private-pool app records the name you enter, your optional email address, the squares you claim, claim time, and payment-confirmation status.

Who can see it

Your name, claimed squares, and payment status are visible to anyone who has the board link. Your email address is visible only to authorized commissioners. Treat a board link like a private invitation and do not repost it publicly.

Why it is used

Commissioners use this information only to run the pool, confirm payments, contact participants about the board, and determine payouts. The app does not process payments or sell participant information.

Correction and deletion

Ask the commissioner who shared the board to correct or release a claim.
